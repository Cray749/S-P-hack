# Data sources, licences and caveats

All data in this repository is public or synthetic. No confidential, proprietary or client data
(including anything belonging to S&P Global or Crisil) is used.

| File | What it is | Source / licence |
|---|---|---|
| `corpus/news.jsonl.gz` | ~5k financial news **headlines** (title, publisher domain, date, URL), 2020-04-09 to 2020-07-16 | FNSPID (Dong, Fan, Peng, 2024, arXiv:2402.06698), **CC BY-NC 4.0**. Headline-level sample only; non-commercial use. |
| `corpus/social.jsonl.gz` | ~16k public stock-market tweets, same window | `StephanAkkerman/stock-market-tweets-data` on HuggingFace, **CC BY 4.0**. Replayed as a historical stream; no live X/Twitter access is used. |
| `analogues/episodes.yaml` | 36 historical episodes with the author's own factual one-line descriptions | Author-written from public record |
| `analogues/library.json` | Market shocks measured for each episode | Computed from Yahoo Finance daily prices via `yfinance` (personal/research use) |
| `market/*.json` | Betas and a credit-sensitivity calibration | Computed from the same prices |
| `entities.csv` | Company master: aliases, parents, promoter groups, industries | Author-compiled from public knowledge; ratings are **synthetic assumptions** |
| `book/book.json` | Synthetic wholesale banking book | Generated by `tremor.book` (seeded); **not real exposures** |

## Known caveats (also in docs/LIMITATIONS.md)

* News timestamps are **day-resolution**. We assume mid-session (15:00 UTC) publication, so the data cannot
  support claims about minute-level lead/lag between news and social posts.
* Social posts carry no author id, so social volume is capped as corroboration and can never confirm an event alone.
* Company names in the synthetic book are real public companies used only as labels; amounts, ratings and
  instrument terms are invented.
* Delisted names (e.g. Silicon Valley Bank, Credit Suisse) have no price history; those episodes contribute
  market, sector and peer evidence but no direct-hit evidence.
